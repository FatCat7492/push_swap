In this chapter, we see the crew of the Heart of Gold
preparing to explore the planet Magrathea. Ford Prefect
expresses his reluctance to bring Marvin, the manically
depressed robot, along on their journey. Zaphod Beeblebrox,
the eccentric captain, dismisses Ford's concerns and insists
they need Marvin, aptly referred to as the "Paranoid
Android."
